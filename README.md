🌱 Planto

Planto is a simple iOS app that helps you keep track of your plants and reminds you when it's time to water them.

Features
Add plants with their name, room, light level, watering schedule, and water amount
Mark plants as watered with one tap
Watered plants move to the bottom of the list, so the ones that still need water stay on top
A progress bar shows how many plants you've watered today
Plants go back to "needs water" automatically based on their schedule
One daily reminder: "Hey! let's water your plant"
Tap a plant to edit it, or swipe to delete it
An "All Done" screen when every plant is watered
An onboarding screen the first time you open the app
